The [trade study](TRADE_STUDY.md) follows the request through provider preparation, transmission, and eligible terminal derivation. Current evidence is a bounded architecture comparison, with one requesting terminal as the principal case. Pooled receivers and independent repeated opportunities are labeled supporting analyses. The historical baseline and current comparison retain the effect of corrected product sizes. A single transfer engine supports both contact interfaces, and catalog-backed diagrams are checked against the manuscript.

The repository workflow includes frozen dependency installation, internal tests, model validation, generated-view checks, local links, exact claim filters, evidence and figure checks, and independent regeneration. These establish internal consistency and reproducibility. Product utility, real service performance, operational interfaces, resource feasibility, and user validation remain outside the evidence.

The [draft](../paper/manuscript_draft.md) has been reviewed from the request through sufficient availability and interface consequences. The [research design](RESEARCH_DESIGN.md) records the causal boundaries, pairing, and uncertainty interpretation. A [targeted literature synthesis](reference/LITERATURE.md) supports the architecture claims, identifies established precedents, and bounds the contribution. It is not a systematic review or evidence of an unprecedented method.

The optional [renderer](../paper/render/README.md) produces a reading PDF and self-contained HTML. Its CI job parses every reference diagram with Mermaid and rejects manuscript diagrams below approximately 8 pt at page width. The corridor lifecycle is a scoped catalog-derived view; the full lifecycle and prior-reference obligation remain in the references. The review corrected Mermaid guard quoting and semicolon handling, compacted dense labels, and increased result-figure font sizes without changing numerical evidence.

Venue formatting and author submission approval remain later manuscript work. The [submitted abstract](../paper/submitted_abstract.md) remains the unchanged submission record. This repository does not claim submission readiness or operational validation.
